2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your display name, username, email address, and profile photo.
  • Profile Information: Bio, memoji/avatar, and preferences you choose to share.
  • User Content: Posts, photos, videos, comments, reactions, and other content you create within the app.
  • Tribe Information: Data related to tribes you create or join, including tribe names, descriptions, cover photos, and membership.
  • Communications: Messages sent through direct messaging and tribe channels.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the Service, including features used, content viewed, and time spent.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we may collect precise or approximate location data to enable location-based features such as the Vibe Map, checking in to a place, and local weather in Vibe Guides. Section 2.3 explains which services receive your coordinates.

2.3 Third-Party Services

  • Music Services: If you connect Spotify or Apple Music, we may access playlist and listening data to power music-based features. We do not store your streaming credentials.
  • Authentication: If you sign in via third-party providers (e.g., Apple, Google), we receive basic profile information as permitted by your settings.
  • AI Services: EchoDrop uses artificial intelligence to power certain features. When you use AI-powered features (such as the Vibe Guide, mood-based suggestions, or content recommendations), your prompts and relevant context may be processed by the following third-party AI providers:
    • Cloudflare Workers AI: Primary AI provider for text analysis and recommendations. Data is processed in transit and not retained after the response is generated.
    • Groq: Fallback AI provider for complex reasoning tasks. Data is processed in transit and subject to Groq's data processing terms.
    We do not send your personal profile information, messages, or location data to AI providers. Only the specific content you submit to AI-powered features is transmitted. No AI provider is used for advertising or user profiling.
  • Content Safety: Uploaded images are analysed for safety compliance using the HuggingFace Inference API. Image data is sent for classification only and is not stored by the provider after processing.
  • Place Names and Map Search (Nominatim): When you use the Vibe Map or check in to a place, the app sends your location coordinates directly from your phone to Nominatim, the place-name service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, to look up the name of the place (reverse geocoding). When you search the map, the app sends the words you search for. Today these coordinates are sent as precisely as your phone reports them. We do not send your name, username or account details, but because the request comes from your phone, Nominatim also receives your device's IP address. The OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy is at osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.
  • Map images (OpenStreetMap and Esri): To draw the Vibe Map, your phone downloads map images for the area on screen from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, or from Esri for satellite view. These requests show the area you are viewing and your device's IP address, but not your name or account details.
  • Weather (Open-Meteo): If you use Vibe Guides, the app asks your phone for a low-accuracy location and sends those coordinates, rounded to four decimal places, directly from your phone to Open-Meteo to get your local weather. If your phone cannot work out the name of your town itself, the app may also send your coordinates to Open-Meteo's place-name service, as precisely as your phone reports them. We do not send your name, username or account details, but Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address.
  • GIF search (KLIPY): When you search for a GIF, the app sends the words you search for, with a strict content filter, directly from your phone to KLIPY to find matching GIFs. We do not send your name, username or account details, but KLIPY receives your device's IP address.
  • Product Analytics: We use PostHog (EU hosting, eu.i.posthog.com) to understand how features are used so we can improve them. PostHog receives in-app event data (for example: screens opened, features used, and when you sign in) together with your account's internal user identifier, your app version and platform. We do not send your email address, name, messages, or the content of your posts to PostHog. Events are linked to that internal identifier, which means they are associated with your account rather than anonymous. PostHog acts as our service provider and does not use the data for its own purposes or for advertising. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Opt out of analytics, which stops collection on that device immediately.
  • Crash Reporting: We use Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring. Crash data and performance metrics are sent to Sentry's servers and are not linked to your identity.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the purposes below. Data protection law requires us to have a legal reason, called a "lawful basis", for each one. The lawful basis we rely on is shown under each purpose.

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including the location features you choose to use
    Lawful basis: to provide the Service you signed up for (performance of our contract with you). If you are under 18, we rely on our legitimate interests in providing the Service you asked for instead. Improving the Service relies on our legitimate interests in making EchoDrop work better.
  • Personalise your experience, including content recommendations and the discovery feed
    Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in showing you content you are likely to find relevant.
  • Enable social features such as tribes, posts, reactions, and messaging
    Lawful basis: performance of our contract with you. If you are under 18: our legitimate interests in providing the Service you asked for.
  • Send you notifications related to your account and activity
    Lawful basis: performance of our contract with you. If you are under 18: our legitimate interests in providing the Service you asked for. You can turn notifications off in your device or app settings.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud or abuse
    Lawful basis: our legitimate interests, and those of other users, in keeping EchoDrop safe, and our legal obligations where the law requires us to act.
  • Comply with legal obligations
    Lawful basis: compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Conduct analytics to understand how our Service is used
    Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in understanding how EchoDrop is used so we can improve it. You can opt out at any time (see "Product Analytics" in section 2.3).

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object (see section 6).

7. Children's Privacy

EchoDrop is only for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up. If we find that an account belongs to someone under 16, we lock the account and then delete it. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with personal data, please email privacy@getechodrop.com.

Accounts of users aged 16 and 17 have extra protections, described in our Child Safety Standards.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Specific retention periods:

  • Account data (profile, email, preferences): Duration of account + 30 days after deletion request
  • Messages: Duration of conversation. Unsent messages are removed from view but retained for safety investigations for 90 days.
  • Vibe Capsules: Auto-expire after 24 hours, or until manually deleted
  • Content (posts, voice notes, articles): Duration of account or until user deletes
  • Safety data (moderation logs, contact rate logs): 90 days
  • Grooming/safety reports: 24 months (legal obligation for child protection)
  • Crash reports & diagnostics: 90 days
  • Analytics events: 12 months, after which they are deleted or aggregated so they no longer identify an individual account

Account Deletion Timeline

When you request account deletion:

  1. Days 1-7 (Grace Period): Account marked for deletion. You can cancel and recover your account.
  2. Day 7 (Soft Deletion): Account deactivated. Profile hidden, content removed from feeds.
  3. Day 37 (Hard Deletion): All personal data permanently removed from primary database.
  4. Day 67 (Backup Purge): Data removed from encrypted backups.

After deletion, we retain only: anonymised safety flags, grooming reports (legal obligation), and aggregated analytics (no personal identifiers).
