Child Safety Standards

EchoDrop · Last updated: October 28, 2026

EchoDrop has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page sets out our standards against CSAE, how to report it, what we do when we find it, and how to contact us. It describes what EchoDrop does today.

1. Prohibited conduct and content

The following are strictly prohibited on EchoDrop and are grounds for immediate, permanent removal from the service:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form: images, video, audio, text, or links.
  • Any content that sexualises a minor, including material that is drawn, computer-generated, AI-generated, or otherwise synthetic.
  • Grooming: building trust with a minor in order to sexually exploit or abuse them, including requests for secrecy, isolation from friends or family, sexualised conversation, requests for images, offers of gifts, attempts to move the conversation to another platform, and attempts to arrange an in-person meeting.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share a minor's intimate images.
  • Trafficking, solicitation, or advertising the sexual exploitation of a minor.
  • Inappropriate contact with a minor by an adult, whether or not it is explicitly sexual.

These rules also appear in our Community Guidelines and in our Terms of Service, which everyone agrees to when they create an account.

2. Who can use EchoDrop

  • EchoDrop is for people aged 16 and over.
  • Everyone gives their date of birth when they sign up. The app works out the age on the phone and sends us only the age band; the date of birth itself is not stored.
  • On phones that support it, the app can also ask Apple or Google for the account's age range. You can decline. If that answer is younger than the date of birth given, we use the younger one.
  • If an account is found to belong to someone under 16, it is locked.

3. Extra protections for users under 18

Accounts of users under 18 get these protections. They are set and enforced on our servers, not only in the app:

  • Their profile is always private.
  • Only friends can send them direct messages or vibe drops.
  • They start hidden from search, and their online status is hidden.
  • Adults cannot find them in people search unless they are already connected, they are left out of other people's follower and following lists, and they are never suggested to anyone as someone to follow.
  • Vibe Swap, the feature that matches people who do not know each other, is for adults only.
  • If we do not know someone's age, people they have not connected with cannot find them in people search or start direct messages with them.

4. How to report CSAE

  • In the app: use Report on the person's profile, in a conversation, or on the post, capsule or comment. Report reasons include grooming, contact with someone under 18, and intimate images.
  • Block and mute: anyone can block another user at any time, which stops that person contacting them.
  • Without an account: you do not need an EchoDrop account to report. Email legal@getechodrop.com with CHILD SAFETY in the subject, and include what you saw and the username if you have it.

We never tell the reported person who reported them. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

5. How we act

  • Review. We aim to review reports within 24 hours. Reports about a child at risk, grooming, or intimate images go to an urgent queue and are looked at first.
  • Intimate images. When someone reports an intimate image, it can be hidden straight away while we review it.
  • Automated checks. These help us find problems, but they can miss things and can be wrong, so they never replace reports:
    • Uploaded photos are checked for nudity by an automated image check, and images it flags as explicit are blocked. If the check cannot run, an upload may still go through.
    • The app checks some text for words and phrases that break our rules before it is posted.
    • On the phone of a user under 18 who has downloaded the app's optional on-device safety model, recent messages are checked on the phone for known grooming patterns. If it finds one, the phone sends us only the type of pattern, a confidence score and which conversation it was in, never the messages themselves. These signals go to our urgent review queue.
  • Human review. An automated signal is never treated as a conclusion. A person on our team confirms every child sexual abuse case before we act on it.
  • Enforcement. When child sexual abuse material is confirmed, we permanently ban the account (a banned account cannot post, message, or sign in), remove the content, preserve the evidence for at least one year (including if the account is later deleted), and report it to the relevant authorities.
  • Notice and appeal. We tell people when we act against their account and how to appeal. In child sexual abuse cases the notice is deliberately general.

6. Law enforcement

Law enforcement and child-protection organisations can contact us at legal@getechodrop.com. Please mark urgent requests CHILD SAFETY in the subject line.
